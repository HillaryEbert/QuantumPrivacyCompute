## Overview

Revolutionary quantum computing platform that combines the power of quantum algorithms with advanced privacy protection through Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE). Experience secure quantum computations on encrypted data without compromising confidentiality.

## Core Concepts

### Privacy Quantum Computing
Our platform introduces a groundbreaking approach to quantum computing where all computations are performed on encrypted quantum states. This ensures that sensitive quantum information remains protected throughout the entire computational process.

### Confidential Quantum Applications
Built on Zama's FHE technology, enabling:
- **Encrypted Quantum States**: All qubit amplitudes are encrypted before processing
- **Private Quantum Algorithms**: Execute complex quantum algorithms without revealing intermediate states
- **Secure Multi-Party Quantum Computing**: Multiple users can collaborate on quantum computations without exposing their private data
- **Zero-Knowledge Quantum Proofs**: Verify quantum computations without revealing the underlying quantum information

## How It Works

1. **Quantum State Encryption**: Users initialize quantum states with encrypted amplitudes using FHE
2. **Algorithm Selection**: Choose from various quantum algorithms optimized for encrypted computation
3. **Secure Execution**: Quantum circuits are executed on encrypted data without decryption
4. **Privacy-Preserving Results**: Computation results are returned in encrypted form, maintaining privacy
5. **Blockchain Verification**: All operations are recorded on-chain for transparency and auditability

## Security Architecture

- **End-to-End Encryption**: Quantum data remains encrypted throughout the computation pipeline
- **Zero-Knowledge Operations**: Computations verified without revealing sensitive information
- **Blockchain Immutability**: All quantum operations permanently recorded on Ethereum
- **Decentralized Trust**: No single point of failure or data exposure
- **Client-Side Security**: Private keys and sensitive data never leave the user's browser
